## ✨ What's Inside VibeHop 2.0

**VibeHop** is a pocket-sized, flow-state arcade runner crafted in a single vanilla HTML5 Canvas + Web Audio API file. Zero external dependencies, zero CDNs, and zero asset bloat—just synthesized lo-fi beats, glowing geometry, and forgiving rhythm-platforming.

- 🎵 **Beat-Synced Visuals & `+50 PERFECT` Timing (`VH-01`)** — Horizon lines, neon pillars, and jump rings pulse in real time to the quarter-note beat; jumping or dashing on-beat awards bonus `⚡ PERFECT VIBE` popups.
- 🎹 **Dynamic Audio Stem Layering (`1x` → `8x` VIBE) (`VH-02`)** — Real-time Web Audio synthesizer opens its low-pass filter from muffled `680Hz` to crisp `2500Hz` as your multiplier climbs, layering snares, swing hats, and a `5x+` fever pentatonic arpeggiator.
- 🛤️ **Elevated Neon Synth-Rails (`VH-06`)** — Lock onto glowing upper rails after jumps to grind across hazards in a shower of amber sparks.
- 📼 **3-Goal Tape Deck Missions & Vibe Ranks (`VH-08`)** — Complete *Alto's Odyssey*-style micro-goals (*"Collect 5 Prism Orbs"*, *"Grind 2 Elevated Neon Rails"*, *"Land 6 On-Beat PERFECT Jumps"*) to level up your persistent **Tape Rank**.
- 🎨 **4 Unlockable Cassette Biome Palettes (`VH-07`)** — Switch anytime between **Midnight Mint** (`115 BPM`), **Sakura Vapor** (`110 BPM`), **Amber Dusk** (`120 BPM`), and **Matcha Rain** (`106 BPM`), each with its own chord progression and CSS/Canvas palette.
- 🧘 **Zen Flow Mode (`VH-05`)** — Toggle **ZEN MODE (∞)** for infinite shields and zero-stress endless cruising.
- 📤 **One-Tap Share Vibe Card (`VH-04`) & Android Haptics (`VH-03`)** — Share your score, peak multiplier, and Tape Level via the native mobile share sheet, complete with tactile vibration feedback on jumps, ring dashes, and shield breaks.

## 📲 Install as an App (PWA)

1. Open **[https://bigknoxy.github.io/vibehop/](https://bigknoxy.github.io/vibehop/)** on your phone or desktop browser.
2. **Android / Desktop Chrome:** Tap the glowing **📲 Install App** button at the bottom of the screen.
3. **iOS Safari:** Tap **Share `[⎋]` → Add to Home Screen** for a full-screen, status-bar-free arcade experience.
